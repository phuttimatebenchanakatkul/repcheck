Everything in one app

Six things RepCheck does that your notes app doesn't

Each one works on its own. Together they close the loop between what you lift, what you eat and what you do next week.

AI form analysis

Film a set on your phone. RepCheck tracks your joints through every rep and tells you which one broke down, why, and the cue that fixes it — not a generic "keep your back straight".

Photo & barcode food logging

Point the camera at your plate and get calories and macros back. Scan a barcode for anything packaged, or search a library of 744 foods that actually includes Thai dishes instead of guessing at "noodle bowl".

A coach that adjusts weekly

Set a goal weight and a date. The coach sets your calories and macros, then rewrites them each week from what actually happened on the scale — not from a formula it ran once at signup.

Workout logging & split planner

Tell it your days, your equipment and your experience, and it builds the split. Log sets as you go, and every session feeds the same picture your coach is reading from.

HYROX race prep

All eight stations plus the runs, with your personal bests, a projected finish time and where you sit on the leaderboard. Log a real race result and see exactly which station cost you.

Friends, challenges & streaks

Add training partners by QR code, run a challenge against them, and let the streak counter do the nagging. The part that keeps you logging in week three, when motivation has left.

FAQ

Questions worth answering up front

What do I actually need to record a form check?

A phone and somewhere to prop it. Film the set side-on, whole body in frame, in normal gym lighting. No wearables, no markers, no special camera.

Is this a replacement for a coach?

No — and we won't pretend otherwise. It's the feedback you don't get on the six days a week you train alone. If you already have a good coach, RepCheck gives them better data to work from.

What does it cost?

Early access is free. When pricing lands, everyone on the waitlist hears about it first — we'll be specific in that email rather than vague here.

What happens to my videos and photos?

They're processed to produce your analysis and stored against your account so you can compare over time. Only what you deliberately share to a leaderboard or challenge is visible to anyone else.

Do I have to use all of it?

No. Plenty of people will only ever use form checks, or only the food log. Each part stands alone; they just get more useful pointed at the same week.

When is launch?

The app is already running with real users in early access. The waitlist is how we open it wider without falling over — join and you'll get a slot in the next batch.
