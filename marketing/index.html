05 — Questions worth answering
What do I need to record a form check?

A phone and somewhere to prop it. Film side-on, whole body in frame, normal gym lighting. No wearables, no markers.

Is this a replacement for a coach?

No — and we won't pretend otherwise. It's the feedback you don't get on the six days a week you train alone.

What does it cost?

Early access is free. When pricing lands, the waitlist hears first — we'll be specific in that email rather than vague here.

What happens to my videos and photos?

They're processed to produce your analysis and stored against your account. Only what you share to a leaderboard or challenge is visible to anyone else.

When is launch?

The app is already running with real users in early access. The waitlist is how we open it wider without falling over.
