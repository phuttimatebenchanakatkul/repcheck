★ The anatomy of a week ★

Every rep scored against the real standard.

Not a vibe check. RepCheck grades depth, tempo and lockout against the movement’s actual criteria, then ranks the fixes by what will move your numbers first.

  1. 01

    Film the set

    One phone, any angle you can prop. No markers, no gym rig, no second person.

  2. 02

    Get the report

    Rep-by-rep scoring against the movement’s standards, with the fixes ordered by impact.

  3. 03

    Next week changes

    The plan rewrites itself from what you actually lifted and ate, not what you planned to.
