RepCheck's AI coach, the workout chat and the Analyze chat answer questions about training, recovery, mobility and nutrition. They are instructed to base their answers on established exercise science, and every answer lists the sources it draws on, with links, directly under the reply.

This page collects every source those answers can cite, so you can read the evidence for yourself.

Not medical advice

RepCheck gives general fitness information, not medical advice, a diagnosis or treatment. AI answers can be wrong. Talk to a doctor or other qualified health professional before starting a new training or diet programme, especially if you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or are recovering from an injury. Stop exercising and get medical help if you feel chest pain, dizziness, shortness of breath beyond normal effort, or sharp or lasting pain.

Calorie and macro targets

Your nutrition targets are calculated with a fixed formula, not by the AI: resting energy use from the Katch-McArdle equation (based on your weight and estimated body fat), multiplied by a standard activity factor, with protein set per kilogram of lean body mass in the ranges recommended by the International Society of Sports Nutrition and Helms et al. (2014).
