RepCheck is a training app. This page explains what it collects, why, who it is shared with, and how to get rid of it.

What we collect

  • Account details — your email address and display name. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email, name and profile picture from Google; we never receive your Google password.
  • Training data — workouts, sets, reps and weights, body weight, HYROX race times, streaks and challenge results.
  • Nutrition data — the meals you log, including photos of food and scanned barcodes.
  • Lift videos — the clips you upload for form analysis.
  • Progress photos — the check-in photos of your body, if you choose to take them.
  • Usage data — which pages you open and how often, used to understand what parts of the app are worth building on.

Health and body data

Body weight, progress photos and lift videos are health-related and are treated as the most sensitive data in the app. Progress photos are private to your account: every route that serves or deletes one checks it belongs to you first. They are never shown to other users, never used in leaderboards and never used for advertising.

AI processing by third parties

Some features work by sending your content to third-party AI providers:

  • Food photos and meal descriptions are sent to Google (Gemini) to estimate the food and its macros.
  • Lift videos are analysed for form; frames and pose data may be sent to Google (Gemini) or OpenAI depending on the analysis.
  • Coach and chat messages are sent to the same providers to generate replies.

These providers process the content to return a result. We do not sell your data to anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored on our servers, hosted with Render. Uploaded lift videos are deleted right after analysis — only the resulting scores and feedback text are kept, plus the trimmed clip if you saved the analysis.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete account, inside the app. Deletion is not instant: your account is scheduled for deletion and permanently erased after {{ grace_days }} days. During that window you can log back in and cancel it from the same screen. After the {{ grace_days }} days, your account and everything attached to it — training logs, nutrition logs, progress photos, saved analyses, custom foods and exercises, friends, challenges and race times — are permanently erased and cannot be recovered.

Your rights

You can view and correct your account details in Settings, and you can delete individual logs, photos and saved analyses at any time without deleting your whole account.

Children

RepCheck is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated date at the top of this page changes with it.
