Common questions

  • I can't log in. If you signed up with an email and password and can't remember it, tap Continue with Google or Continue with Apple on the login screen using that same email address — it opens your existing account with all your history. Two cases where it won't: if the provider doesn't confirm that address as yours, and if you use Apple's Hide My Email (the relay address isn't the one your account is under). You'll land in a new empty account rather than an error, so if your training history isn't there, stop and email us instead of signing in again.
  • I want to delete my account. Go to Settings → Delete account, inside the app. See our Privacy Policy for details on the deletion window.
  • A camera or upload flow isn't working. Email us with your device model and iOS version and we'll look into it.
  • I found a bug or have feedback. Email us — screenshots or a short description of what you were doing help a lot.
  • Someone's name or behaviour is offensive. Tap the ⋮ next to them on a leaderboard or in your friends list to report or block the account. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours, and reporting blocks them for you straight away. You can email us about anything the in-app report doesn't cover.

See also our Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Cookie Policy and Refund Policy.
